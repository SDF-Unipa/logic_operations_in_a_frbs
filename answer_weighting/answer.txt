QUESTION: What is current state of European Energetic transition?

ANSWER: Europe’s energy transition is progressing steadily, driven by rapid growth in renewables, electrification, and stronger climate policies. Wind and solar now supply a significant share of electricity, while dependence on fossil fuel imports has decreased since the 2022 energy crisis. However, challenges remain: grid modernization, energy storage, industrial decarbonization, and uneven progress among member states. High investment costs and regulatory complexity continue to slow deployment in some sectors. Overall, Europe remains a global leader in the energy transition, balancing energy security, affordability, and climate objectives while accelerating toward net-zero targets by 2050.
